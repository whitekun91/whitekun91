### About Me

Industrial Engineering background (**Kyung Hee University**). I enjoy connecting **domain knowledge** with **data**, finding the story in between, and shipping models that actually solve problems—not only generative AI, but classical **ML**, **forecasting**, **computer vision / 3D metrology**, and **manufacturing & quality** use cases.

Several years of experience as an **ML engineer** and **data scientist** in R&D and industry (smart manufacturing, solution SW). Comfortable with **team workflows** (Jira, Confluence) and **Git**-based collaboration.
